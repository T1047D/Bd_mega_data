OUR DATA PROMISE

Useful information.
Responsible by design.

BD MEGA DATA is designed to make public information easier to discover while respecting privacy, source attribution and dataset reuse conditions.

Our data principles →
01
Source transparency

Every published dataset should identify where it came from.

02
Privacy first

No private citizen profiles, home addresses or sensitive personal records.

03
Verification matters

Unverified submissions stay out of the verified directory.

04
Open where permitted

Reuse only data whose terms allow the intended publication.
